Behind the frequency

About.

I’m MACKINTHEDARK. Radio Meltdown is a space for music, independent voices, culture and creative expression. This is where the music plays, the conversations happen and the signal stays independent.

SanecaTV Radiomeltdown is an independent broadcast network operating out of the G4E Movement Studio in Lithonia, Georgia, under the Saneca TV LLC umbrella. Established in 2021, it has been a platform for indie artists, underground talent and community voices from day one.

Our mission is simple: give independent artists a platform, give the community a voice, and keep the frequency live.

Probate calls are slower and softer than a standard wholesale cold call. You're talking to someone who may be grieving. Lead with condolences, not offers.

1 · Opening & Condolence

▶
YouHi, is this [Executor/Heir Name]? My name is [Your Name] — I'm sorry to bother you, and I want to say I'm sorry for your loss.
ThemThank you... who is this?
YouI work with local families going through probate on [Property Address]. I wasn't sure if the property was something the family planned to keep, sell, or needed help managing — no pressure either way, I just wanted to reach out.

2 · If Open to Selling

▶
ThemWe have talked about selling it, honestly.
YouThat makes sense — a lot of families don't want to deal with repairs or listing during probate. I buy houses as-is, so there's no cleaning out, no repairs, no realtor fees. Would it help if I gave you a no-obligation offer once you have a minute to think it over?
YouWhat's the timeline looking like on the estate — are you past the court process, or still early?

3 · Common Pushback

▶
ThemIt's too soon, we haven't decided anything.
YouTotally understand — no rush at all. Would it be alright if I checked back in a few weeks, once things have settled a bit?
ThemHow did you get this information?
YouProbate filings are public record through the county court — I reach out respectfully in case it's helpful, not to pressure anyone.
ThemWe're planning to keep it in the family.
YouThat's great to hear. If it ever needs cleanup, repairs, or upkeep before anyone moves in, I also work with a cleaning/landscaping crew — happy to leave my number just in case.

4 · Pivot to Service Opportunity

▶
YouNo problem at all if selling isn't the plan. A lot of families in probate just need the property cleaned out or the yard maintained before it's sold, rented, or moved into — is that something that would be useful right now?
ThemActually yeah, it's been sitting empty and the yard's a mess.
YouI can get you a quote for a cleanout or landscaping this week, no obligation. Should I text or email you the details?

5 · Closing / Do Not Contact

▶
YouThanks so much for your time, and again, I'm sorry for your loss. I'll leave you my number in case anything changes.
If they ask to be removed from contact — mark the file "Do Not Contact" immediately in the docket and end the call politely. Never call back a DNC lead.

THE BASIC METHOD

Turn a probate filing into a real estate lead.

  1. Find the estate. Search recent probate filings.
  2. Identify the property. Determine whether the deceased owned real estate.
  3. Find the representative. Locate the executor, administrator, or personal representative.
  4. Research the property. Check ownership, estimated value, liens, mortgage information, condition, and equity.
  5. Qualify the opportunity. Look for properties where selling, cleanout, repairs, landscaping, or management may be needed.
  6. Add it to your docket. Use the NEW FILE tab above to track the lead.
IMPORTANT: Probate records are public records, but information and availability vary by county. Verify the property, ownership, representative, and contact information before outreach.

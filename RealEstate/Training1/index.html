Training 01

Find
Motivated Sellers.

Stop looking and sounding like every other wholesaler. The goal isn't simply more marketing. The goal is getting noticed.

The question everyone asks
“How do I find motivated sellers?”

But nobody wants to admit the real problem…

Everyone is marketing the exact same way.

  • Texts people ignore
  • Calls people don't answer
  • Postcards that go straight in the trash

And then people say:

“It's a numbers game.”

It's not.

It's a you-look-like-everyone-else problem.

Think about it.

If a seller is getting hit by 10 wholesalers…

and all 10 wholesalers say the same thing…

why would that seller respond to you?

Don't just optimize the same strategy.

Instead of trying to optimize the same scripts and channels, you need to be different enough to actually get noticed.

Simple Example

Instead of sending a normal postcard, send one that simply says:

“I made a video for you.”
No pitch. No long explanation. Just enough curiosity to make them stop.

Now the seller might think:

“Wait… what video?”

That's the point.

Instead of ignoring you, they go check.

When they do, they land on a simple page with a short video where you introduce yourself and give them a way to reach out.

No pressure.

One small shift can change the response.

You don't necessarily need:

  • A completely new script
  • A bigger list
  • More marketing volume

You need something that makes the seller stop and pay attention.

CASE STUDY 02
EVENT OPERATIONS / SYSTEM

Hit For Hit Battles

Hit For Hit Battles needed a more organized way to plan and run their events. The team needed to track talent and staff without relying on constant phone calls and messages, and fans needed a simple way to vote, see battle information, and access the event — all while keeping the system simple for a team that isn't highly tech savvy. SENSTACK built a connected event ecosystem that separates the public-facing experience from internal event operations, cutting down on repeated calls, scattered information, and "who's doing what?" confusion.

Built for events. Adaptable to other industries.

CASE STUDY 03
MUSIC / DIGITAL PRODUCT

MACKINTHEDARK — Music Preview System

An independent artist wanted to promote music online without giving away entire tracks for unrestricted free listening. The real problem wasn't "how do I play music on a website" — it was how to let people experience enough of the music to want more without handing over the complete product. SENSTACK built a custom listening experience around controlled music discovery, turning the artist's site into part of the promotional strategy instead of a basic player.

Controlled music discovery without giving away the complete product.

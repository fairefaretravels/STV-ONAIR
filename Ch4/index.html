About The Educational Mix

The STV Educational Video Mix is an original Saneca TV editorial format that connects music, artists, video and cultural history. Each mix introduces viewers to artists and songs while providing context about the people, places, sounds and stories behind the music.

The goal isn't simply to play videos. It's to help viewers discover artists, understand their influence and explore the culture surrounding the music. STV combines curated music programming with original commentary, artist stories and visual storytelling to create an educational experience for music lovers, students, creators and fans.

    Issue 02
    Tony T Money Green
    Detroit Bass Legend · Issue 02 Cover

    Before Snoop Dogg laid his first verse. Before Tupac recorded All Eyez on Me. Before Dr. Dre built the sound that defined a decade — there was a bass player from Detroit named Tony "T Money" Green, and without him, none of it sounds the way it does.

    "He gave me drumlines and I put basslines on top. My bass was the groundwork. That was a song."
    — Tony T Money Green on working with Dr. Dre

    The story starts in Detroit, 1975. L.J. Reynolds of The Dramatics spotted a young Tony Green playing at a local club. After one audition, Tony had the bass chair for over 20 years — co-writing their last top 10 hit and in 1978 co-writing "One of Those Funky Things" for Parliament. Then in 1993, he became band director at Death Row Records — powering Doggystyle, All Eyez on Me, and the Grammy-winning "Let Me Ride" remix.

How to play

Make your case

  • One host gets 30 seconds to defend the first position.
  • The other host gets 30 seconds to defend the opposing position.
  • Each host gets one challenge question.
  • Finish with a follow-up that makes both hosts think deeper.

No automatic winner. Let the hosts disagree naturally.

Producer's note

Keep it interesting

Ask for real reasons, examples, and nuance. Challenge sweeping claims without turning the conversation into a personal attack.
